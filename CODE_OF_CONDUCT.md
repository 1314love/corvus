# Code of conduct / 行为准则

[English](#english) | [中文](#中文)

---

## English

### Scope

This repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, commits, reviews. Also anyone speaking for the project elsewhere.

### Expected

- Disagree on technical grounds, with evidence.
- Assume good faith until proven otherwise.
- Keep reports reproducible: version, steps, logs.
- Credit work you build on.
- Accept a maintainer decision and move on.

### Unacceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, sexualised attention.
- Discrimination based on identity.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Spam or off-topic promotion.
- Disruption that continues after a request to stop.

### Report

Email `cosher@163.com`, subject `Corvus conduct`. Include links, dates and what happened. Reports stay confidential: the reporter is not named to the other party without consent.

### Response

- 5 business days: acknowledgement.
- Steps, scaled to the case: clarification, private warning, temporary ban, permanent ban.
- Content that breaks this policy is removed or hidden immediately.

### Appeal

Reply in the same thread. A different maintainer decides where possible.

### Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions that break this policy, and may block accounts.

## 中文

### 适用范围

本仓库：issue、pull request、讨论、提交、代码评审。也适用于在其他场合代表本项目的人。

### 期望行为

- 就技术本身争论，给出证据。
- 未证实前默认对方善意。
- 报告要可复现：版本、步骤、日志。
- 引用他人工作时注明出处。
- 接受维护者结论，继续推进。

### 不可接受

- 骚扰、侮辱、威胁、性化关注。
- 基于身份的歧视。
- 未经同意公开他人隐私信息。
- 灌水或离题推广。
- 被告知停止后继续扰乱。

### 举报

邮件 `cosher@163.com`，主题 `Corvus conduct`。附链接、时间与经过。举报保密处理：未经同意不向对方透露举报人。

### 处理

- 5 个工作日内确认收到。
- 措施按情节递进：澄清、私下警告、暂时封禁、永久封禁。
- 违反本准则的内容立即删除或隐藏。

### 申诉

在同一邮件线程回复，尽可能换一位维护者复核。

### 执行

维护者可编辑、隐藏或删除违反本准则的内容，并可封禁账号。
