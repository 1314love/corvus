## Limitations

- The clean NFR-3 72-hour soak was stopped at the client's request after about eight hours (last checkpoint 8 at 2026-11-14T22:41:37Z). NFR-3 is not passed and 1.1.0 is not 72-hour soak certified.
- Firefox 121+ remains unsupported; the NFR-6 deviation is open and option A is recommended.
- Linux packaging is x86_64 only; macOS has no packaged installer.
- The daemon has no authentication; keep ports 7330/7331 on loopback.
- The live service was intentionally left on 1.0.0 because the user's normal Edge browser was connected. The 1.1.0 installer was verified on a throwaway service instead of disrupting that browser.
